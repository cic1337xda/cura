Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Last revised: October 2026 • Cura. Hospital Management Information System

1. Data Collection & Purpose

Cura. HMS collects patient personal data, medical diagnoses, vital signs, prescriptions, laboratory results, and staff authentication details solely for medical records administration, healthcare delivery, and hospital inventory tracking.

2. NFC & Smart Card Data

NFC hardware identifiers (including standard 8-character CUID HEX tokens and physical MyKad RFID chip UIDs) are utilized exclusively as cryptographic index pointers to link patients to their authorized digital medical files on the hospital database.

3. Confidentiality & Access Controls

Medical records are restricted via role-based access control (RBAC). Only authenticated healthcare practitioners and system administrators possess authorization to view, record, or update patient health information.

4. Session Tokens & Security

Authentication sessions are secured using industry-standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT). All system communications occur over TLS-encrypted channels upstream.

5. Compliance

Cura. HMS complies with local patient data protection directives and Malaysia Personal Data Protection guidelines (PDPA) regarding medical history storage and retention.
